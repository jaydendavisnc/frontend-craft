# Make the tool feel made

10 moves. Loud ones first within each source. A page holds two or three loud moments at most.

## `bar-behind-row`

Ranked list where each label sits on a tinted bar scaled to its value: 36px pitch, 32px bar, radius 4, 10% tint, value right aligned at 14/500 in its own column. Bars stop short of the values. One tint per panel.

- Build: Read `references/elements/app.md` lines 444 to 463.

## `bump-chart`

Rank over time: smooth curves across 5 to 6 named columns, one highlighted in ink and the rest pale, rank labels at left, category pills above. Curves are safe here because only order matters. Say rank, not value.

- Build: Read `references/elements/app.md` lines 1353 to 1362.

## `chromeless-product-canvas`

No window. A tinted panel about 845px wide is the app: oversized search line in display type, a hairline, then 4 masonry columns of 170px with a 16px gap mixing photos, quotes, highlighted notes and price tags.

- Build: Read `references/elements/product.md` lines 334 to 343.

## `composer-close`

The last call to action is the product: centred 48px heading, then a real 54 to 62px input, up to 766px wide, radius 14, with its action inside. Prompt box, URL field, file drop or preset amounts. Starts the first task instead of describing it.

- Build: Read `references/elements/section-conversion.md` lines 1190 to 1208.

## `direct-label-chips`

Lines labelled on the plot, no legend: filled 12/600 chips on 1px vertical leaders into each curve, single pale axes, 11px caps axis titles. Highlight series dashed in accent with an open-ring terminus and a bordered note. State when schematic.

- Build: Read `references/elements/app.md` lines 1236 to 1253.

## `embedded-working-product`

Real working UI inline with no frame: a 1136px search with four selects, a count and a pager; or 52x76 date chips with masked ends; or a typed block with a caret. Full size, keyboard reachable, and it must work on a phone.

- Build: Read `references/elements/product.md` lines 606 to 615.

## `quiet-text-filter`

Filtering set as plain text. One centred 32px serif sentence of comma-separated categories, or ALL and STYLE at 13px on a rule one tile wide, or 12px header links with the active one underlined. For editorial sites and small catalogues under 30 items. Never for large ranges.

- Build: Read `references/elements/commerce.md` lines 1023 to 1030.

## `quote-beside-interaction`

A review shares a row with something the reader can do: two 546 by 600 cards, 8px gap, radius 24, one a 44px question with 54px pill answers, the other stars, 44/48 quote and a member chip. Proof sits one tap from the decision. Keep legal disclosures visible.

- Build: Read `references/elements/section-proof.md` lines 490 to 497.

## `scrubbable-slow-motion`

A sentence appears word by word on a rule with per-token numbers under it, a candidates list beside it, and a scrubber labelled 30x slowed down, from Detailed to Realtime.

- Build: Read `references/elements/product.md` lines 2033 to 2042.

## `search-as-navigation`

Search or a single question is the hero, for sites people come to find something in: 85px band with 24px placeholder, 42-56px pill search with filter chips, two cards with search and date, or a lone 1px underline prompt. No marketing claim.

- Build: Read `references/elements/hero.md` lines 1737 to 1745.
