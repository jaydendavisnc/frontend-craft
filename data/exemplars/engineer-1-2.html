Postgres error codes, exactly as they appear in your logs.

These five errors also bring people to me

53300

Too many connections

The app scaled out and Postgres did not. PgBouncer in transaction mode fixes most of it. The ORM settings that break afterwards are the real work.

55P03

Locks during a deploy

One ALTER TABLE queues behind a long transaction, then the site queues behind it. I rewrite migrations into steps that hold locks for milliseconds.

53100

Disk full

Usually an abandoned replication slot keeping WAL forever. I drop it, add the alert, and find out what it was for before anyone recreates it.

40001

Serialization failures

Two jobs keep fighting over the same rows. Changing the access order often ends it, and retries go in only where the work is safe to repeat.

XX001

Corrupted pages

Checksums failed after a storage fault. I work out which pages are gone, rebuild what WAL can rebuild, and tell you plainly what cannot come back.
