Getting here, and where to sleep

Every crossing runs on the same tide table. Your ticket comes with a printed copy, so plan the drive around it.

Crossing the bar

Hadlock Bar is 0.6 miles of gravel, and it is dry for about three and a half hours around each low water. The shuttle stops 20 minutes before the road floods, and it will not wait for you.

By car

Park at the Grange field in Steuben, $10 a day, and take the free shuttle across while the road is open. Private cars stay off the bar. Steuben is 90 minutes from Bangor on Route 1.

By bus from Bangor

Route 1 Coach runs $38 each way from Bangor airport, leaving at 7:15 am on Thursday and returning from the island at 1:00 pm on Monday. Book it with your ticket.

By water taxi

If you miss the road, the taxi runs from Milbridge town wharf at high water, $14 each way, until 1:00 am. It carries 12 people at a time and no bikes.

Step-free access

Accessible shuttles cross in every road window. The yard and the Packing Floor are level, and beach wheelchairs are free at the Bar. The Net Loft is up 16 stairs and has no lift.

Sleeping on the island

If you camp, you are on the island from Thursday morning, and the tide stops being your problem. Hotels in Milbridge and Ellsworth sell out by March.

Tent pitches

Every Weekend with camping pass gets one 10 by 10 ft pitch on the west field, first come, first pitched. The gate opens with the road at 9:13 am on Thursday.

Water and showers

There are six taps on the west field and a washhouse with 14 cold showers. The hot ones are $4 a token, and the line is shortest during the headliner.

Food on the island

Eleven local stalls in the cannery yard, including lobster rolls at $24 and a vegan chowder that people drive back for. The whole island is cashless, and you top up at the token booth.

Phone signal

One bar at the south end of the wharf and none on the west field. There are charging lockers at the token booth for $6 a day.
